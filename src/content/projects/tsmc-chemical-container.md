My senior capstone at Washington State University Vancouver, sponsored by TSMC Washington.

## What I did

- Designed a 3D-printed polypropylene chemical transport container with integrated safety features for hazardous fluid handling.
- Made sure the design met OSHA regulations and TSMC's internal safety standards for hazardous chemical handling.
- Used rapid prototyping, including 3D-printed snap-fit features and plastic welding, to iterate on and validate working prototypes.

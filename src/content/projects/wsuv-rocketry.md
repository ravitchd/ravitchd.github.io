I worked on the WSU Vancouver Aerospace Club's rocket for the 2022 Spaceport America Cup.

## What I did

- Developed and modeled the rocket's main body, working across several teams to make sure everything assembled properly.
- Ran finite element analysis on the airframe in SolidWorks.

## Result

The team placed **3rd in category and 6th overall** at Spaceport America Cup 2022.

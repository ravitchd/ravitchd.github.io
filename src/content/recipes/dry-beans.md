---
title: Dry Beans
category: technique
tags: [beans]
date: 2026-09-30
notes:
  - 4.5 oz of dry beans = 1 can (15 oz).
steps:
  - Measure out dry beans and pick through to remove any that are split, wrinkled, or any rocks.
  - Rinse beans until water is clear.
  - Put beans in a pot on the stove with water just covering them. Add some salt, pepper, paprika, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder and a cut up slice of raw bacon.
  - Heat the water to a boil and simmer the beans for 3-4 hours, adding water as it gets absorbed, and adding salt to taste.
  - Beans are finished when they squish easily between fingers. I like to cook until just before finished for adding to chili.

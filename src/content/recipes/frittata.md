---
title: Frittata
category: savory
tags: [eggs, baked]
date: 2026-10-02
ingredients:
  - 10 eggs
  - ⅓ cup milk
  - 4-cheese blend, shredded
  - Block of havarti, cubed
  - Veggies
  - Sausage or bacon
steps:
  - Cook meat.
  - Chop vegetables and cube havarti.
  - "Mix eggs and milk in a bowl and add spices to liking: salt, pepper, onion powder, garlic powder, dill, thyme, oregano."
  - Sprinkle half of the havarti in the bottom of a 9x13 dish.
  - Layer half of the vegetables.
  - Drown everything in the 9x13 dish in egg mixture.
  - Layer the meat and other half of vegetables.
  - Sprinkle remaining havarti and shredded cheese on top.
  - Bake at 350°F for 25-35 minutes.

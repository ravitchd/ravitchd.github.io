---
title: Lasagna
category: savory
tags: [pasta, baked]
date: 2026-10-03
ingredients:
  - 1 pound ground beef
  - 28 oz jar of spaghetti sauce
  - 1 egg
  - ½ tsp oregano
  - ½ tsp basil
  - 1-2 packages uncooked lasagna noodles
  - 16 oz sour cream
  - 16 oz cottage cheese
  - 2 cups shredded cheese
  - ¼-½ cup diced onion
steps:
  - Preheat oven to 375°F.
  - Brown ground beef in a pan and add chopped onion. After cooked, add spaghetti sauce and simmer.
  - In a bowl combine cottage cheese, sour cream, egg, oregano and basil.
  - "Layer in a 13x9 dish: meat mixture, noodles, sour cream mixture, cheese, meat mixture, noodles, sour cream mixture, cheese."
  - Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes then take foil off and bake for 15 more minutes.

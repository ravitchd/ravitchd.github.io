---
title: Jimmy Dean and Rice
category: savory
tags: [rice, baked]
date: 2026-10-01
ingredients:
  - 1 cup dry rice
  - 1 package Jimmy Dean
  - ½ cup shredded cheese
steps:
  - Cook the rice.
  - Cook Jimmy Dean on the stove.
  - In an oven safe pan, spray with oil and lay the rice on the bottom. Salt and pepper as desired.
  - Layer the Jimmy Dean on top of rice.
  - Sprinkle the cheese over.
  - Bake at 350°F until the cheese melts.

---
title: Salmon Patties
category: savory
tags: [seafood]
date: 2026-10-01
makes: 4 patties
ingredients:
  - 1 pound pink salmon
  - 3-4 heaping tablespoons greek yogurt
  - 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  - 1 tablespoon flour
  - Dill, salt, and pepper
steps:
  - Peel skin off salmon and debone.
  - Mash salmon in a bowl.
  - Add yogurt, lemon juice, flour, dill, salt, and pepper to salmon and mix.
  - Form 4 patties from mixture and cook in pan until internal temp is reached.

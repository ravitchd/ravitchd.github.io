---
title: Tini Mac and Cheese
category: savory
tags: [pasta, baked]
date: 2026-10-08
ingredients:
  - 12 oz pasta
  - 2 oz sharp cheddar
  - 4 oz colby jack
  - 2 oz mozzarella
  - 2 oz gouda
  - 1 ½ tbsp butter
  - 1 ½ tbsp flour
  - Salt and pepper
  - 1 ½ tbsp garlic powder
  - 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  - 6 oz evaporated milk
  - 8 oz heavy cream
  - ½ tbsp dijon mustard
steps:
  - Preheat oven to 350°F.
  - Shred the 4 cheeses and combine into a bowl.
  - Cook pasta in salted water according to package.
  - Combine garlic powder, smoked paprika, and salt and pepper to taste in a small bowl.
  - Melt the butter in a pan then add half of the seasonings.
  - Add the flour and whisk for 1-2 minutes, the butter and flour mixture should be bubbling.
  - Add the evaporated milk and stir, once it begins to thicken add the heavy cream, remaining spices, and dijon mustard.
  - Add half of the cheese mixture a little at a time stirring it in after each addition.
  - Add cooked pasta to the sauce and combine.
  - Transfer half of the pasta to a baking dish, layer ½ of remaining cheese, layer the rest of the pasta, and cover top in remaining cheese.
  - Bake for 10-15 minutes then broil top for 1-2 minutes.

---
title: Focaccia
category: bread
tags: [yeast]
date: 2026-10-06
versions:
  - label: Full batch
    steps:
      - Whisk together 2 tsp of yeast in ½ cup of warm water. Wait for it to get foamy.
      - Add 5 cups of flour, 2 cups of water, and 2 tsp of salt. Mix until a sticky and wet dough forms.
      - Cover with a towel and let sit in a warm spot for 1 hour then mix the dough again.
      - Pour dough into a sheet pan and stretch until it covers the pan.
      - Cover with a towel and let sit another 15 minutes.
      - Drizzle with olive oil and dimple the dough, sprinkle flaky salt on top.
      - Bake at 425°F for 25 minutes.
  - label: Half batch
    steps:
      - Whisk together 1 tsp of yeast in ½ cup of warm water. Wait for it to get foamy.
      - Add 2 ½ cups of flour, ¾ cup of water, and 1 tsp of salt. Mix until a sticky and wet dough forms.
      - Cover with a towel and let sit in a warm spot for 1 hour then mix the dough again.
      - Pour dough into a sheet pan and stretch until it covers the pan.
      - Cover with a towel and let sit another 15 minutes.
      - Drizzle with olive oil and dimple the dough, sprinkle flaky salt on top.
      - Bake at 425°F for 25 minutes.

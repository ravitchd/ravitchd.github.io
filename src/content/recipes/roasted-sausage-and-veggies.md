---
title: Roasted Sausage and Veggies
category: savory
tags: [sheet pan]
date: 2026-10-02
ingredients:
  - 2 packages mini chicken apple sausages
  - 1 zucchini
  - ½ butternut squash
  - 3 gold potatoes
  - ¼ white onion
steps:
  - Heat oven to 375°F.
  - Dice veggies into bite sized pieces.
  - Cut sausage into bite size pieces.
  - "Spread everything onto oven safe oiled pans: potatoes and squash may go on the same pan, sausage and onion may go on the same pan, zucchini on another pan."
  - Salt and pepper the squash.
  - Salt, pepper, garlic powder, and italian seasoning on the potatoes and zucchini.
  - Drizzle olive oil over everything.
  - Cook the squash and potatoes for 30 minutes, sausage and onion for 15 minutes, and zucchini for 20 minutes.

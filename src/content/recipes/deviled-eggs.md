---
title: Deviled Eggs
category: savory
tags: [eggs, party]
date: 2026-10-01
versions:
  - label: 18 eggs
    ingredients:
      - 18 eggs
      - 9 tablespoons mayonnaise
      - 3 tablespoons Dijon mustard
      - 3 tablespoons white vinegar
  - label: 24 eggs
    ingredients:
      - 24 eggs
      - 12 tablespoons mayonnaise
      - 4 tablespoons Dijon mustard
      - 4 tablespoons white vinegar
  - label: 30 eggs
    ingredients:
      - 30 eggs
      - 15 tablespoons mayonnaise
      - 5 tablespoons Dijon mustard
      - 5 tablespoons white vinegar
steps:
  - Cook and peel hard boiled eggs.
  - Cut eggs lengthwise and separate yolks into a bowl.
  - Mash the egg yolks.
  - Add the mayonnaise, mustard, and vinegar to the egg yolks, salt and pepper to taste.
  - Whisk mixture until smooth.
  - Scoop or pipe mixture into the hollowed out eggs and sprinkle with paprika for garnish.

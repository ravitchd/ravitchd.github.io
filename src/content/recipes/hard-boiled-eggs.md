---
title: Hard Boiled Eggs
category: technique
tags: [eggs]
date: 2026-09-30
steps:
  - Bring water to a boil on the stove with a splash of white vinegar.
  - Gently place eggs into the boiling water.
  - Reduce to low boil/simmer and put the lid on.
  - Let cook for 14 minutes (8 for soft boiled).
  - Transfer eggs to ice bath for 15 minutes.

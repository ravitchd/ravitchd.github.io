---
title: Bagels
category: bread
tags: [yeast]
date: 2026-10-05
versions:
  - label: Full batch
    makes: 8 bagels
    steps:
      - Whisk together 1 tbsp sugar, 2 ½ tsp yeast, and 2 cups of warm water. Wait for it to start foaming.
      - Add ½ cup melted salted butter and yeast mixture to a stand mixer with a dough hook and gradually add in 4 cups of flour and let knead for about 10 minutes. When dough is sticky gradually add another cup of flour.
      - Cover dough with towel and let sit for about an hour until it doubles in size.
      - Cut into 8 pieces, roll into a ball and shape the bagel.
      - Let formed dough sit covered for another 10-15 minutes or until they puff up again.
      - Boil water with 1 tbsp of honey or molasses and a couple tablespoons of baking soda. Boil the bagels 40 seconds per side.
      - Egg wash the top of the bagels and add toppings.
      - Bake for 15 minutes.
  - label: Half batch
    makes: 4 bagels
    steps:
      - Whisk together ½ tbsp sugar, 1 tsp yeast, and 1 cup of warm water. Wait for it to start foaming.
      - Add ¼ cup melted salted butter and yeast mixture to a stand mixer with a dough hook and gradually add in 2 cups of flour and let knead for about 10 minutes. When dough is sticky gradually add another ½ cup of flour.
      - Cover dough with towel and let sit for about an hour until it doubles in size.
      - Cut into 4 pieces, roll into a ball and shape the bagel.
      - Let formed dough sit covered for another 10-15 minutes or until they puff up again.
      - Boil water with 1 tbsp of honey or molasses and a couple tablespoons of baking soda. Boil the bagels 40 seconds per side.
      - Egg wash the top of the bagels and add toppings.
      - Bake for 15 minutes.

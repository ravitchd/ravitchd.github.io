My senior capstone at Washington State University Vancouver, sponsored by TSMC Washington: a container for holding and transporting hazardous liquids, modeled in SolidWorks and 3D printed in polypropylene.

- Integrated safety features for hazardous fluid handling, designed to meet OSHA regulations and TSMC's internal safety standards.
- Prototyped with 3D-printed snap-fit features and plastic welding to iterate on and validate working prototypes.
